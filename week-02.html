Architecture Briefs · Week 2

Permission to Forget

Memory is not only what happened. It is what remained addressable. Reconstruction treats forgetting as a cut in the record — a withdrawal of speech — not as soft decay. When a line of structure is pulled from language, the next generation inherits the gap as if it were empty from the start. The architecture did not vanish. Access to it was closed.

The official story prefers amnesia that looks natural. An intentional cut implies a cutter. Naming the cut would reopen who held permission to edit the record — so the blank is sold as ordinary time.

What did you train yourself to call “just how things are” that was once a decision?

— Bjorn Aeolus
