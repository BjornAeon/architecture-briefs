Constructed Origin

The official origin story treats beginning as settled — a creation event that closes the question. Reconstruction starts one layer earlier: at the agreement that a constructed sequence can be called origin. Structure precedes the story that claims to explain it. What gets remembered as “how it began” is often the first successful edit of the record, not the first event. Once that edit hardens into doctrine, the blueprint underneath stops being asked about.

Official histories need a clean start. A constructed start implies authorship, and authorship implies someone who can revise the blueprint. That is why the gap is left blank rather than investigated — not because nothing sits there, but because looking would reopen the record.

Where did you agree to treat a constructed story as origin?
